Who should buy first

The right early customer has a QA pain.

  • Radiology group with high reporting volume and pressure on turnaround time.
  • Hospital department trying to standardize report review and amendment visibility.
  • Imaging operator willing to run retrospective validation before live deployment.
  • Clinical leader who wants measurable accept/edit/reject evidence before budget approval.
