What to expect

How long is it free?
Throughout early access. There is no announced end date. Future pricing will be communicated in advance; this is not a promise of free access forever.
Is processing unlimited?
No unlimited usage is promised. Processing capacity and availability are limited. We’ll confirm the report volume and workspace scope with you before onboarding. There are no automatic paid overages during free early access.
How many users can join?
Tell us your team size in the access request. We’ll confirm the initial workspace setup with you.
Are custom integrations included?
The free offer covers early-access product use. PACS, RIS, LIS and EHR connections need a separate technical assessment; custom development and third-party services are not promised as part of this offer.
Will this turn into a paid subscription?
There is no automatic paid conversion through the access request. Any future paid offer will be explained separately.
