The clinical problem

Give every draft a structured review before sign-off.

A report can contain conflicting sides, missing comparison context or inconsistent findings and impressions. Med-AI brings potential issues and their source evidence into the clinician’s review workflow.

See the report and the evidence together.

Review draft sections, inspect potential laterality conflicts and comparison gaps, then decide what to correct before signing.

Learn from clinician feedback.

Rate useful alerts and record report edits. Use those observations to evaluate your team’s experience rather than assume that every suggestion helps.

Keep review actions attributable.

Audit trails, clinician attribution, critical result workflows, consent models, and FHIR-oriented export are already part of the product architecture.

Product story

From draft to reviewed report.

Review report sections, check evidence-linked alerts and record a clinician’s decision. Explore the workflow with synthetic cases before requesting a team workspace.

Prepare the draft

Turn a study and draft report into a structured review item with sections, urgency labels, and evidence for the clinician to inspect.

Review the risks

Surface laterality conflicts, missing prior comparisons, critical result acknowledgements, and report quality issues before final release.

Sign and learn

Capture accept, edit, or reject decisions as quality data for model evaluation, clinician governance, and future rollout planning.
