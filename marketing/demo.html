01 / Interactive clinical demo

Find the discrepancy. Make the correction.

Choose a radiology or laboratory case, compare the supplied observations with the draft, then edit FINDINGS, COMPARISON and IMPRESSION.

  1. Read the synthetic source. Each teaching draft intentionally contains a reporting issue.
  2. Run demo QA. Inspect the case-specific consistency checks and their explanations.
  3. Edit and rerun. Check your revision, view an example, or reset the exercise.
  4. Share the case. Each visitor starts with the original draft; your edits are not included in the link.

The public demo uses local teaching rules, not an AI model or the production QA engine. Edits stay in page memory. Passing these checks is not clinical clearance.

SYNTHETIC TEACHING EXAMPLE

One observation. Two conflicting statements.

Supplied observation

Small right pleural effusion.

Draft with intentional errors

Findings: Small left pleural effusion.

Impression: No pleural effusion.

Illustrative correction

Findings: Small right pleural effusion.

Impression: Small right pleural effusion.

The revision aligns the side and impression with the supplied fictional observation. This illustration is not a published clinician-reviewed case.

02 / SIGNED-IN WORKSPACE

Feedback inside the report workspace

Flag a wrong suggestion, explain what was missed, and submit a proposed correction linked to a saved report.

  • Use the selected QA suggestion as context.
  • Review saved feedback with its clinician, timestamp and report snapshot.
  • Keep feedback separate from clinical editing and sign-off.

Available to Doctor and Hospital Admin accounts. Feedback does not automatically change a report or train a model.

05 / SIGNED-IN PILOT RESULTS

Evidence with visible denominators

Assess recorded centre activity over a selected UTC date window, with sample sizes and definitions alongside the results.

  • Useful / not-useful QA ratings and rating coverage.
  • Saved editing revisions and explicit EDITED sign-offs.
  • Median and 90th-percentile creation-to-signature time.
  • Active staff adoption and aggregate JSON export.

Turnaround includes waiting time; revisions are an effort proxy. Missing data is marked “Not measured.” No baseline means no claim of time savings or clinical accuracy.
