Screenshots

Explore the reporting workflow.

These images were captured from the local app using synthetic/demo data. They show representative workflow states. Sample metrics are illustrative, not measured customer outcomes.

01 Dashboard

Your workspace at a glance.

The dashboard explains the operating layer: patient volume, file volume, analysis status, QA access, platform health, and fast routes into worklist and clinical workspace.

  • Useful for hospital administrators and pilot owners.
  • Links to worklists, reports and review tools.
  • Sample values demonstrate the interface; evaluate real activity in your workspace.
02 Clinical Workspace

Report and evidence, side by side.

The reviewer sees report text, issue severity, supporting evidence, anatomy mapping, and the sign-off state in one place.

  • AI drafts and flags. Clinicians decide.
  • Review laterality, comparison and criticality, then edit or sign.
  • Use the live demo for a labelled synthetic teaching case.
03 Worklist

The governance layer hospitals expect.

The worklist gives teams a queue, escalation visibility, and a path from draft to reviewed report.

  • Shows pending and signed reports.
  • Highlights critical result acknowledgement.
  • Keeps patient-facing release tied to clinician action.
04 QA Analytics

Review the activity behind your evaluation.

Review whether clinicians accepted, edited or rejected drafts. Interpret activity alongside case mix, rating coverage and a separately measured baseline.

  • Tracks clinician agreement and corrections.
  • Connects product use to validation metrics.
  • Read the evaluation page for metric definitions and limitations.
05 Integrations

Confirm each connection before use.

Connection status helps your team distinguish configured interfaces from integrations that still require setup and testing.

  • Confirm PACS and RIS interfaces with your IT team.
  • SSO is required for larger hospitals.
  • Do not market integrations as live until they are actually connected.
