Validation

A pilot plan that creates buyer evidence.

The next stage is not more feature spread. It is a focused retrospective validation pilot that proves report QA value on real workflow data under clinician supervision.

Why this matters

The product needs proof, not bigger promises.

A hospital or imaging group will buy when the product can show fewer QA misses, faster review handling, measurable clinician agreement, and clean governance. The pilot is designed to collect that evidence safely.

Metric

Clinician agreement

How often drafts are accepted, edited, or rejected before signature.

Correction categories

Metric

Operational lift

Eight-week pilot

A focused validation path.

Weeks 1-2

Workflow mapping and data scoping

Agree modalities, report types, review process, PHI boundaries, and success metrics.

Weeks 3-4

Retrospective run

Run selected historical studies through the QA workflow with synthetic or de-identified data where possible.

Weeks 5-6

Clinician review

Radiologists or quality reviewers accept, edit, reject, and annotate output inside the governed workspace.

Weeks 7-8

Evidence report

Deliver pilot metrics, issue categories, workflow recommendations, and production-readiness gaps.
