What the workspace dashboard measures

Signed-in workspace results use recorded activity and a selected date range. No fabricated success percentages or customer logos are used.

MeasureRecorded evidenceInterpretation
Alert usefulnessQA runs, alerts, rated alerts and useful / not-useful clinician ratings.Show rating coverage and counts. Unrated alerts are not useful ratings; multiple reviewers may rate an alert. This is not sensitivity or specificity.
Editing activityDraft-save events, distinct edited reports and signed reports marked edited.Activity is a proxy for effort, not a count of corrected errors or a measure of typing time.
Reporting elapsed timeMedian and 90th-percentile time from review creation to signature, for eligible signed reports.Includes waiting and interruptions. It is not active clinician time or measured time saved.
AdoptionDistinct participants with tracked workflow events during the chosen period.Participation does not establish retention, clinical benefit or a share of all eligible users.

A practical evaluation plan

  1. Agree the scope

    Choose report types, supported inputs, reviewers, usage capacity and data-handling arrangements.

  2. Define a baseline

    Record a comparable review workflow before evaluation. Document dates, sample size, exclusions and case mix.

  3. Review both flags and misses

    Clinicians inspect source evidence, rate alerts, correct reports and separately assess missed issues. Include reports without alerts.

  4. Report counts and limitations

    Share denominators, rating coverage, elapsed-time definitions and unresolved issues. Publish a customer case study only with permission and measured evidence.
